ABOUT BLOOD DONATION

A little kindness can mean everything.

Blood donation helps hospitals provide care to patients during emergencies, operations, and medical treatments. Voluntary donors play an important role in keeping blood supplies available.

  • Support people who need blood.
  • Encourage voluntary blood donation.
  • Build a caring and informed community.
